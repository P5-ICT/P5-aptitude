# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Participants:** Pillar 5 talent and candidates completing an 84-question aptitude assessment (~30 minutes). They need clarity, low friction, and confidence that their responses matter.
- **Staff:** Pillar 5 HR and talent development team (`@pillar5group.co.za`). They review submissions, scores, and top-3 role recommendations in a protected admin dashboard.

## Product Purpose

The Pillar 5 Aptitude Test helps participants discover their top three role-family pathways through automatic scoring. Staff use the admin UI to review outcomes stored in Airtable. Success means participants complete the assessment without confusion and staff can trust the data at a glance.

## Brand Personality

**Confident, institutional, clear.**

Voice is direct and professional — a trusted assessment tool, not a marketing funnel. The landing introduces Pillar 5 with authority; the test flow stays calm and readable; admin surfaces are dense and functional.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing pages (purple gradients, hero metrics, icon-card grids)
- Glassmorphism and decorative blur cards
- Cream/sand body backgrounds that read as AI-default warmth
- Uppercase tracked eyebrows on every section
- Ghost cards (1px border + wide drop shadow)
- Playful or casual assessment UIs that undermine institutional trust

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — especially in the test wizard and admin; every element earns its place.
2. **Brand at the threshold** — landing and header carry Pillar 5 identity; in-flow screens serve the task.
3. **Progressive disclosure** — one section, one purpose; participants always know where they are.
4. **Accessible by default** — contrast, focus states, and reduced-motion alternatives are not optional.
5. **Institutional confidence** — typography and spacing signal seriousness without sterility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1.
- Full keyboard navigation for forms and test options.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected for all animations.
- Clear error messages and required-field indicators on forms.
